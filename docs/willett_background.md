# Willett Family Estate Background

## Historical Context

### Willett Distillery Restart

The Willett Distillery (Kentucky Bourbon Distillers, Ltd.) restarted distillation operations in **2012** after decades of sourcing whiskey from other distilleries. The barrel number ranges documented in this application refer specifically to **Willett's own post-2012 distillate**.

### Pre-2012 Sourced Whiskey

Before restarting distillation, Willett Family Estate bottlings were primarily sourced from other Kentucky distilleries:

- **Bourbon Sources**: A large portion of early Willett Family Estate bourbon releases (especially older age statements before 2012) are widely believed to have been sourced from **Heaven Hill Distillery**.

- **Rye Sources**: Early Willett rye releases came from multiple sources:
  - Some young rye whiskey was sourced from **MGP (Midwest Grain Products)** in Indiana before Willett's own rye matured.
  - Extremely old rye releases (such as the legendary 25-year Family Estate Rye) are believed to be from older **Heaven Hill/Bernheim rye stocks**.

### Barrel Number Coverage

**Important**: The barrel classification system in this app covers **only Willett's own distillate** (post-2012). It does **not** fully cover:

- Pre-2012 sourced barrels
- Older age-statement releases bottled before Willett began distilling
- Barrels from other distilleries that Willett may have acquired

### Source Distillery Attribution

For all barrel ranges documented here:

- `sourceDistillery`: **"Willett Distillery"** - These are Willett's own distillate, produced at the Willett Distillery in Bardstown, Kentucky.

### Note on Age Statements

This database does not attempt to track barrel ages or distilled years. Age statements and vintage information vary by release and are typically found on individual bottle labels or release documentation.

## Mashbill History

Willett has experimented with various mashbills since restarting distillation in 2012:

1. **High Corn Low Entry** (79/7/14 @ 103 proof) - Discontinued, used in earliest distillate
2. **High Corn High Entry** (79/7/14 @ 125 proof) - Active, higher proof version of high corn
3. **Original Bourbon** (72/13/15 @ 125 proof) - Core bourbon mashbill, most widely used
4. **Wheated Bourbon** (65/20/15 @ 115 proof) - Wheated alternative with experimental variations
5. **High Rye Bourbon** (52/38/10 @ 125 proof) - Bold, spicy bourbon with high rye content
6. **High Rye Rye** (74/11/15 @ 110 proof) - Flagship rye whiskey mashbill
7. **Low Rye Rye** (51/34/15 @ 110 proof) - Gentler, sweeter rye whiskey
8. **Four Grain Bourbon** (55/12/18/15 @ unknown proof) - Experimental four-grain recipe

## Barrel Finishing & Experimental Releases

Some barrel ranges include special finishing or experimental treatments:

- **Hoffmeister Barrels** (86xx range) - Rye aged in barrels from the German Hoffmeister cooperage
- **Cured Oak** (253xx range) - Experimental wheated bourbon in cured oak barrels
- **Char Variations** (383xx range) - High rye bourbon with Char 1 barrels
- **American Oak** (321xx range) - Four grain bourbon in American oak

## Unknown Mashbills

Some barrel ranges (such as 771x) are documented but the specific mashbill remains unconfirmed. These are marked as **"??? mashbill bourbon"** in the database to indicate uncertainty while preserving the known barrel range information.
